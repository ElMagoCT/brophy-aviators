How to fly
the sim.

On the monitor
1
Sign in
Tap your name. First time? Type first name + last initial under New pilot and press Add & sign in
On the TV
2
Fly
Press FLY → THE TV and take the yoke. The sim un-pauses and your time counts.
Optional
3
Want VR?
Switch the headset on, press TURN ON VR, put it on and sit still for the 10-second countdown.
Finished
4
Done flying
Back at the monitor, press DONE FLYING. Time saved, flight logged, sim paused for the next pilot.
Good to know
  • Time paused (idle) — no yoke, key or mouse for 30 s. Move anything to carry on.
  • Signed out after 3 idle minutes, time saved first.
  • The board ranks hours this week and resets Sunday at midnight.
  • Lab hours follow the school day; it shuts while a class is in the room.
